# Inter-Rater Protocol (Draft)

**Status:** draft v0.1. This protocol will be tested and revised during the Month 2 pilot, before full scoring begins.

## Purpose

To check whether SCARCE-Bench scores are reliable and not the judgment of a single person.

## Raters

Independent specialists and community knowledge holders. Raters are compensated and take part by informed consent.

## Procedure

1. Raters are trained on the rubric using a small set of practice outputs.
2. Outputs are scored with model identity hidden where practical.
3. A defined subset of outputs is double-coded by two raters. The subset and its size are fixed before scoring begins.
4. Inter-rater reliability is calculated with Krippendorff's alpha.
5. Disagreements are adjudicated and the reasons recorded. Rubric wording is revised where disagreements show ambiguity.

## Targets

- Month 2 pilot: alpha of 0.7 or higher on a pilot set before full scoring begins
- If the target is not met, the rubric is revised and the pilot repeated

## Reporting

Agreement is reported overall and for each failure mode and tradition. Unresolved disagreements are reported, not hidden.

## Community knowledge

Community-held knowledge is used with consent. Communities keep control over how it is used, and anything they ask to keep private stays out of public releases.
